description
A robot can solve physics you can't. It still can't tell you whether the cup on the edge of the table is about to fall.

That isn't ignorance of the law. F = ma fits on a page. Applying it needs the mass, the friction, how far past the edge the cup already is, where it's touching — and none of that is in the equation. The law is never the missing piece. The state is, and getting it is a looking problem, not a physics problem.

What covers the gap is intuition: a rough guess learned from watching. You don't solve equations to catch a ball, and people who draw the wrong path for a ball leaving a curved tube catch it perfectly well. Intuition isn't an alternative to physics — it's a cheap approximation of it.

So a machine needs the world to arrive in some form, and that usually means pixels. But the best world model we have uses no pictures at all: weather forecasting runs on pressure, temperature and wind, sampled across a grid, hour after hour. The camera was never the point. The grid was.

And none of it is the world itself. Pixels, pressure — all measurements. Train a model to guess the next move in a board game, give it move lists and nothing else, and a board appears inside it that nobody put there. A world model is the guess you grow from measurements, because guessing is all you get.

简介
机器人能解你解不出的物理题。可桌边那个杯子要不要掉，它说不上来。

这不是不懂定律。力等于质量乘加速度，一句话就写完了。可要拿它去算，你得知道质量、摩擦、已经探出去多少、哪里接触着——方程里一个都没有。缺的从来不是定律，是状态。而拿到状态，是"看"的问题，不是物理问题。

补上这个缺口的，是直觉：看得多了，很快给出一个粗略的猜测。你接球的时候没有解方程；那些画错弯管轨迹的人，接起球来一个都不差。直觉不是物理的替代品，它是物理的一个便宜又好用的近似。

所以机器得先把世界收进来，通常靠像素。可我们手里最好的那个世界模型，一张图都不用：天气预报靠的是气压、气温、风速，在网格上一小时采一次。关键从来不是摄像头，是那张网格。

而这些都不是世界本身。像素也好，气压也好，都只是测量。训练一个模型，只让它猜棋类游戏的下一步，只给棋谱，别的什么都没有——棋盘却在它里面出现了，没人放进去。世界模型，就是你从测量里长出来的猜测，因为你只有猜测。

1. tell (whether / if) — 看出来、分辨（to know something by looking — not 'say'）
2. that's not what's missing — 缺的不是这个（the thing you named isn't the problem）
3. fall back on — 最后只能靠（to use when nothing better is available）
4. a rough guess — 粗略的猜测（approximate, good enough, not exact）
5. all you get — 你只有这个（there is nothing else available）
