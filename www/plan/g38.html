G-38 — the half-mile card

Episode 1, scene 07 — the anchor that answers "why are we seeing these places". Three pins drop as they are named, then the walk draws between the first two. Deliberately almost empty: the argument is that the dots are close together, and every extra mark makes that harder to see.

What it does not claim. The three addresses are real and sourced. The line between two of them is a schematic tracing of the streets the research names, not a survey — so the card carries no street names, and it labels the walk with Verne's step count rather than with the tracing's own length. The tracing is ~1,085 m; Fogg's 1,151 steps work out at roughly 900 m; the real streets are about a kilometre. Three different numbers, and only the step count is a fact about the novel.
