The card generator — 18 cards, one grammar

Episode 1 asked for twenty-one generated assets that are mostly one date, one number, or two labelled things. Twenty-one bespoke files would have produced twenty-one slightly different type scales, which is how a house look dies. Instead: lib/cards.mjs, one card.json per asset, and a type scale that lives in exactly one place.

Six types, chosen from what the episode actually needs:

  • lines — a stack of lines, revealed in order
  • figure — one enormous number, plus a caption
  • timeline — dated points along an axis
  • measure — a line with two ends named and a distance
  • pair — two labelled things side by side
  • compare — labelled bars against each other

Everything reveals on the narration clock, like the map and the record board. A locale can override any string on any card by key, so a half-translated Mandarin cut degrades to mixed rather than to blank.
