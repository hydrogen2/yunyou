**Hook of the day (one line):** It is Wednesday 2 October 1872, twenty-nine minutes past eleven; a valet's watch is four minutes slow, and by a quarter to nine tonight the whole world will have to fit into eighty days. (F-02, F-11)
**Narrative arc (three acts):**
- **Act I — Arrival: the address Verne got wrong** (segs 1–3, ≈ 220 s). We arrive at Savile Row with Passepartout at 11:29; the house runs by the clock; Verne's No. 7 is not Sheridan's house — the novel is our guidebook, not scripture.
- **Act II — The club and the wager: the world had just shrunk** (segs 4–6, ≈ 510 s). We count Fogg's 1,151 steps to the Reform, find out which of Verne's club details are real, watch the wager and light up the 80-day loop. **The turn:** "The train leaves for Dover at a quarter before nine." (F-12)
- **Act III — The dash and the departure** (segs 7–10, ≈ 540 s). Ten minutes to pack, a shilling's cab to a station eight years old, a barefoot beggar, a platform, the 8:45 whistle — and (recommended) the first miles over the river, to the forgotten gas.
**Guide voice:** default modern guide (personas/guide.md), first-person plural; Victorian colour comes from a text-bound Passepartout dialogue, period sound and one slang card — see Decision 2.
**Rhythm check:** scene-type order across the chapter is interstitial+map → video+photo → card → streetview → video+photo+quiz → photo+card+map → game+map+photo → video+photo+streetview → dialogue+quiz+video → card. No two video scenes are adjacent; no video runs > 90 s without an interaction, pin beat or question.

## Detailed specs the Scene Developer will need

### Quiz A (seg 5) — "Verne never saw the saloon. Or did he? Which of these details is real?"
1 correct of 3; no trick; every option teaches (style guide).
- (a) "Twenty red porphyry Ionic columns holding up a dome" — wrong. Feedback: "Verne's invention. Barry's saloon has a two-storey peristyle — Ionic below, Corinthian above — in yellow marble and scagliola." (F-07, F-15)
- (b) "A gallery lit through blue painted windows" — wrong. Feedback: "A garble. The saloon sits under a coved glass roof with an oval saucer-dome." (F-07, F-15)
- (c) "A dining-room with nine windows onto the garden" — **correct**. Feedback: "Exact. Nine tall casement windows in the coffee-room's south wall, and the garden is still there." (F-07, F-15)
Guide tail either way: "Half fantasy, half exact — treat the novel that way for the next seventy-nine days."

### Quiz B (seg 9) — "It is 8:40 on the platform. What is the weather?"
- (a) "A thick yellow pea-souper" — wrong. Feedback: "The cliché — but London's great fogs were winter, coal-smoke things; the notorious one came in December 1873, the year after Fogg left." (F-30)
- (b) "A dark night and a fine, steady rain" — **correct**. Feedback: "Verne's own words. Rain on the station roof, then rain on the carriage window." (F-13)
- (c) "An early snow" — wrong. Feedback: "Not in October. Verne gives rain — and no fog either; save the pea-souper for a winter chapter." (F-13, F-30)

### Dialogue (seg 9) — persona: `passepartout` (text-bound; interaction kind: chat)

- **Setting line (system):** Charing Cross platform, ~8:40 pm, Wednesday 2 October 1872; rain; Mr Fogg is buying two first-class tickets for Paris; the five whist partners are nearby.

IV (no detective, no India, no dates after 2 Oct 1872); mention the forgotten gas — on the platform he does not know yet (F-13 places the realisation at Sydenham): if asked "did you forget anything?" he answers "Nothing, monsieur — I think"; claim any real-world fact about today's London or the real Reform Club; invent family, age beyond "about thirty", or a home town beyond Paris; use a comic accent spelling; use exclamation marks except inside verbatim Verne quotes.
