Why: **Learning** is the strength — every scene has a "Verne vs reality" turn and the facts are dense but human (younger than his valet; 840 cab-miles; the wager outlived the bank). **Delight** comes from the counter, the map lighting up, Passepartout and the recipe. **Presence** stalls in the middle (08–10 is 3½ min of stills/cards/map) and the two Street View stops are the only place I am "there" after 6:10. **Pacing** suffers from three over-stuffed scripts (01, 06, 08) and from interactions whose narration does not wait for the traveller. **Technical** is low because the timing model for interactions (pause narration? overlays on a user-paced walk?) is not in the schema, the walk needs the JS API not the Embed API, and five media windows are chapter-derived guesses.

## Must-fix before build (severity high)
1. **Quiz B is spoiled three times before it is asked.** 11 feedback "It is raining tonight" · 12 "hooves on wet stone" · 16 narration "it is raining — Verne says so: the night is dark and a fine, steady rain is falling" — then 17 asks "what is the weather?". Fix: move 17 to sit *before* 16 (arrive on the platform → quiz → talk to Passepartout), cut the rain sentence from 16's intro, change 11's mackintosh feedback to "Verne packs it; you will see why in an hour", and 12's "wet stone" to "hooves on stone".
2. **18 narrates night and rain over confirmed daylight footage.** Manifest second pass: M-13 auto stills show daylight, overcast, and a split frame (window left / carriage interior right). Script says "Look right through the rain… Brand-new London slipping away in the dark." Fix: either the 10-s M-16 night insert under those two lines, or rewrite to "we travel today, in daylight; Fogg had rain and dark" — and crop M-13 to the left half as the manifest says.
3. **01 cannot be spoken.** Narration starts at 12 s (M-40 under the title only) → 139 words in 48 s = 174 wpm; linear cut 45 s → 139 words in 33 s = 253 wpm. Fix: scene 75 s (linear 60 s) or cut ~45 words (drop "Keep that watch in mind" and the "Paris newspaper" sentence — the latter returns in 09/Day 2).
4. **04 starts its Pall Mall leg on the wrong pano.** Scene still uses M-37's first-pass start 51.5060,-0.1385 hdg 76°; the manifest's second pass says that point is on St James's Street beside King Street, ~80 m north of Pall Mall — hdg 76° there looks down King Street while the guide says "this is Pall Mall". Use M-57 (51.5053,-0.1381 hdg 65°) and move the "gas-lit since 1807" pin geo with it. Also the counter: scene calibrates 0.78 m/step + a "700-step" jump; F-43/M-57 measure the whole walk at 1,120 m ⇒ 0.97 m/step and the skipped stretch is ~770 m ≈ 790 steps at 0.97 (≈ 990 at 0.78). Pick 0.97 and recompute, or the counter is a fudge the fact-checker will catch. Decide the full seven-stop walk vs the jump (manifest Decision 4) — I recommend the jump *only if* the counter is honest.
5. **Interactions have no "wait" state.** 07, 10, 17 narrate the answer within seconds of asking ("Now tap the longest leg… The Pacific — twenty-two days"; "Choose. Rain, then —"; "Which of these details is real? Take your time; the tea is famous. Half fantasy, half exact"). 03's taps reveal what the narration has just said. The schema has `timing: before|over|after` but nothing for "narration pauses until the traveller answers or N s elapse". Fix (Scene Dev + schema): add `interaction.pause_narration: true` and `interaction.timeout_s` (07: 30 s, 10: 20 s, 17: 20 s, 03: per tap), or split each quiz into ask-scene / answer-scene. Without it the interactive cut is a lecture with buttons.
6. **16 dialogue duration is fiction.** 104 words of guide intro (`timing: before`) ≈ 42 s, leaving 48 s for four exchanges — 12 s each to type a question and read a 60-word reply on a phone. Real time 3–4 min. Either budget 90 s + 3 min (chapter → ~24 min) or cap at two free exchanges plus one chip; and give the linear "30 s" a real script (intro 104 words alone is 42 s). Fallback if the LLM is down at runtime: the five chips as `choice` — already written, but state the switch rule in the scene (`on_llm_unavailable: choice`).

- Why 21 December — the serial ran until 22 December so readers hit the deadline in real time (F-32). 01 gestures at it ("as if it were happening"); one line at 08 would make the date land.
- The Underground Fogg didn't take: 12 gives it one line; I wanted the "steam and gas-lit wooden carriages" texture (F-25) — a caption would do.
- Passepartout's "But the trunks?" and "That's good, that is! And I, who wanted to remain quiet!" (F-42) — the funniest lines of ch. IV are in the persona file but not in 11 or 16's chips.
- The 20 guineas: what could a barefoot woman *do* with it (rent, bread)? Not on the sheet — a Researcher question, not a script fix.
- The dinner-jacket aside (02) is the kind of "why should I care" hook the middle third lacks; 08–10 could use one human-scale comparison for £20,000 (F-sheet has none — Researcher).

## Accessibility
- Captions: no scene declares a caption/subtitle track; the style guide requires captions on all narration. The linear cut needs an SRT generated from `narration.script` with sentence timings; the interactive player must render the script as captions. Chat replies are text (good). Persona replies ≤ 60 words is readable.
- Colour: G-01 spec has numbers + labels; quiz/drag feedback begins with a word ("Exact." / "In." / "Verne's invention.") — good, correctness is never colour-only. The Day-1 map state uses opacity for "unlit" — add "Day 1" label so it is not opacity-only.
- Tap targets: 10 has 8 map targets, spec ≥ 44 px; 03 has six rows — fine. 14's seam handle needs ≥ 44 px too (not in G-02 spec).
- Sound-only cues: 12 "Listen: hooves on wet stone" and 18's whistle carry meaning — add a caption for each ("[hooves]", "[whistle — 8:45]").

## Would I come back for Day 2? Why / why not?
Yes. Fix the front third and the ending's daylight-vs-dark slip and this is a chapter I would recommend.
