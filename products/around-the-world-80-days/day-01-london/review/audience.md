# Audience Report #1 — founder playtest of Day 1 (player + watch), 2026-08-19

**Analyst:** (role's first input came directly from the founder)   **Severity:** high — editorial stance, not polish

## Finding
The founder — who IS the target persona (armchair traveler; knows only the title of the novel; non-native English
speaker) — had to read Wikipedia mid-tour to follow Day 1. Two causes:
1. **Literary-companion stance.** The spine assumes the book is known (cold open starts in media res; Act I's central
   joke annotates a text the viewer hasn't read). We annotate the novel instead of retelling it.
2. **Language register.** Literary diction, Victorian vocabulary (wager, valet, whist, guineas, hansom), high fact
   density per minute — steep for non-native speakers.

## Decisions (founder, 2026-08-19)
- **North star for this first production:** the founder personally feels *traveled* and *learned*. That is the
  acceptance test for Day 1 and every chapter after it.
- Adopt **retell-the-story spine + two knowledge layers**: main narration assumes the traveler has NEVER read the
  source and tells the story as we go; connoisseur material (translation trivia, meta-jokes about Verne's errors)
  moves to optional pins / "go deeper" layers. The well-versed keep their layer; it no longer blocks the door.
- Add a **clear-English narration track** (parallel simplified script per scene, selectable at start; slightly slower
  TTS) plus tap-a-word **glossary cards** for period terms (feeds the language-learning strategy).
- **Player aids:** "who's who" card reachable anytime; "previously on…" chapter recap; narration speed control.
- Rejected: a separate "Day 0" prologue (fold the bet-in-one-breath into the cold open instead).
- Full localization (e.g. Chinese narration) noted as the natural extension of the variant mechanism; later.

## Consequence
Style guide rule added (see shared/style-guide.md “Assume no prior reading”); rewrite pass queued BEFORE the linear
re-render so the next video already speaks to newcomers.
