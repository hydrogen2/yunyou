## Email template

> **Subject:** Permission to use ~80 seconds of your London walk in a narrated travel series
>
> Hello — I'm Zhiwei, and I make **Yunyou**, a guided armchair-travel series for people who watch walking videos and
> want a story and some history alongside them. The first episode retraces Phileas Fogg's 1872 departure from London,
> from Savile Row to Charing Cross, in *Around the World in Eighty Days*.
>
> Your video **"[TITLE]"** is the best footage I've found of **[PLACE]**, and I'd like to use
> **[MM:SS–MM:SS, about NN seconds]** of it in the episode.
>
> To be clear about what I'm asking, because there are two different things:
> - In the **interactive version** your video is *embedded* — it plays from YouTube, you keep the views and any ad
>   revenue, and I'm not asking permission for that.
> - I'd also like to publish a **narrated video version** of the episode. That means the clip is inside my file, so I do
>   need your permission — and a copy of the segment from you, since I won't download from YouTube.
>
> What I can offer: your channel named on screen while the clip plays and again in the credits, a link in the
> description, and if you'd prefer a licence fee instead of (or as well as) credit, tell me your rate and I'll consider it.
> The episode is free to watch; there's no paywall and no advertising on it.
>
> If you'd rather not, that's completely fine — I'll keep the embed in the interactive version and use a title card in
> the video instead. And if you'd like to see what it looks like first, I'm happy to send you the current cut.
>
> Thank you for making this footage — it's the reason a project like this is possible at all.
>
> Zhiwei
