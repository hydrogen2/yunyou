# Media sourcing — Episode 1, 2026-09-09

Everything below was found by searching Wikimedia Commons directly (the `action=query` API, not a
scrape), and every licence and pixel size here was read off the file's own `extmetadata`, not guessed.
Under D4 our outputs are CC BY-SA 4.0, so share-alike sources are usable; each is flagged `sa: true`
so the renderer burns its credit while it is on screen.

## Judgement calls worth your veto

**M-202 is a substitute.** The slot asked for an orbital *sunrise* to sit under "sixteen sunrises a day".
Commons has no good one. This is a second night-side frame, so the line now plays over night rather than
over the thing it describes. It holds the beat; it does not illustrate it. Re-source if one turns up.

**M-207 is small — 360×469.** Bisland is thinly photographed. At the capped upscale it plays as a plate on
a mount rather than filling the frame, which is honest but means she is visually the smaller of the two
women in a topic whose point is that there were two of them. The alternative on Commons is 236×673, worse.

**M-224 cannot show four minutes slow.** The dial reads what it reads, and we do not alter photographs.
So the shot is a watch and the *narration* carries the four minutes. The slot note forbids adding hands or
captioning a time.

**M-84 is resolved rather than replaced.** It was mis-briefed in the first cut as containing the Charles I
statue, which it does not. It no longer has to: M-223 carries the statue in the next slot, and M-84's job is
now only "the place, alive, now". ⚠ Its licence is still unconfirmed with the uploader.
