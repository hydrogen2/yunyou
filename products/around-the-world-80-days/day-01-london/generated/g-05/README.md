# G-05 — Memorandum of the wager (scene 08-the-wager, media 30–60 s)

Files: `memorandum-card.svg` (viewBox 1920×1080), PNGs `memorandum-card_1920x1080.png`, `memorandum-card_3840x2160.png`, `memorandum-card_2176x1812.png` (letterboxed on cream).
Rebuild (SVG + PNG, from repo root): `node studio/tools/gen/cards_day01.mjs g-05` — all text lives in the DATA block at the top of `studio/tools/gen/cards_day01.mjs`; the SVGs here are build output, do not hand-edit. PNG export uses `studio/tools/svg2png.mjs` (playwright-core chromium, house fonts from `studio/player/fonts/fonts.css`, `--bg #efe6d3`).

Facts on the card: six signatories in order Andrew Stuart (engineer), John Sullivan (banker), Samuel Fallentin (banker), Thomas Flanagan (brewer), Gauthier Ralph (Bank of England — F-08 "one of the Directors of the Bank of England"), Phileas Fogg last — F-08 (names, trades) + scene ("Stuart first, Fogg last"); stake "twenty thousand at Baring's", "half of his fortune", "eighty days or less", "in this very room of the Reform Club", return Saturday 21 December 1872 · 8:45 pm (R1 numerals; Verne's "a quarter before nine p.m." kept out of the card as it is not quoted), £20,000, "1,920 hours, or 115,200 minutes" — F-11; Fogg's address "No. 7 Savile Row" — F-01; Baring's — F-23; date/place line "Reform Club, Pall Mall — Wednesday 2 October 1872" — F-02/F-11. Quoted phrases are Towle's 1873 translation (PD).

Style: signatures are typeset Playfair Display italic (no handwriting fonts); one accent object — the "80 DAYS" seal — plus the header rule in accent. Sans for the deadline, stake and roles.

Known limits: the memorandum wording ("That Mr Phileas Fogg shall…") is our paraphrase framing Verne's quoted phrases — not a quotation itself. 3840×2160 is a 2× raster of the same 16:9 layout; 2176×1812 letterboxes top/bottom.
