# Cut from Episode 1, kept on purpose

Scenes the founder cut from the episode that are still good writing. They are not deleted because a cut
topic is exactly the shape of a **Short** — self-contained, one idea, its own hook — and shorts are how a
new channel gets found. Anything here can be rendered on its own without the episode.

|------|----------|--:|----------------|-----------------|

It needs no setup and no payoff. |
