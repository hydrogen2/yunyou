## 2 · What does not exist yet

**Nothing.** As of 2026-09-09 every one of the 105 slots resolves to a real asset — 18 outstanding.
The last nine were closed by looking harder rather than by paying: an 1868 *Illustrated London News* engraving of a
Royal Geographical Society meeting (Wellcome, CC BY), the United Service Club's map room from the same 1841 book
that gave us the Reform saloon (Rijksmuseum, CC0), and — the one that dissolved a rights problem rather than
working around it — Palle Huld photographed at the Gare du Nord by a FRENCH agency in 1928, public domain, where
the Danish press photograph could not be cleared.

Three slots became cards, because a card was honestly better than the photograph would have been:
G-54 (the trade's military dates, replacing a uniform plate that does not exist free), G-55 (the Reform's 85,000
volumes, replacing an interior the club will not licence) and G-56 (one gas lamp, 1,920 hours — which is exactly
how long the bet runs, a connection no photograph of a lamp could have made).
