# Brief — Around the World in 80 Days · Day 1: London

**From:** founder   **Date:** 2026-08-18   **Status:** approved (dry run)

## The one-liner
"I think it's nice to have a guided armchair tour that retraces Phileas Fogg's route — starting in the London he
left on 2 October 1872 — mixing today's 4K walking footage with the Victorian city, so I feel like I'm actually setting off."

## Audience & mood
Curious adults who love landscape/walking videos but find pure visuals boring after a while. Fold-phone / tablet, evening,
headphones. Feel: anticipation of departure, cosy Victorian-club warmth, then the thrill of the first train out.

## Must-haves / nice-to-haves / must-nots
- Must: real, embeddable YouTube walking/POV footage of today's London; the Reform Club & the wager; the departure from
  Charing Cross; a map beat showing the whole 80-day route; at least one quiz and one Street View walk; a Verne persona or
  Fogg/Passepartout dialogue if guardrails are solid; a "souvenir".
- Nice: Victorian sound design; a "then vs now" photo pair; some Cockney/Victorian slang.
- Not: horror/crime tourism (Jack the Ripper etc.), politics of the day beyond a line, anything not verifiable.

## Constraints
~18–22 min interactive; ~10–12 min linear cut. English. Public-domain source text (Verne, 1873; Towle/d'Anvers translation OK).
Generated media budget: a few illustrations/maps only.
