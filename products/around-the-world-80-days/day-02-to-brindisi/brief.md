## The one-liner
"The first full day of the wager: London to the heel of Italy in seven legs — and the joke that Passepartout,
who has waited his whole life to see the world, is going round it without seeing any of it."

## The spine (offered to the Rundown Writer, not imposed)
**The man who circles the world and sees nothing of it.** Verne makes this explicit and comic: Passepartout is
carried through Paris and gets minutes of it; Fogg plays cards and does not look out of the window. That tension is
gold for us, because it is the exact inverse of what our traveller is doing — we are the ones who stop and look.
Use it. The chapter's emotional payoff should be that we see what Fogg refused to.

## Must / nice / must-not
- **Must:** the crossing to Calais; why the route goes over the Alps and down to Brindisi at all; **the Mont Cenis
  (Fréjus) tunnel** — opened 1871, one year before Fogg, and the third "the world just shrunk" enabler after Suez
  1869 and Promontory 1869 (Day 1 lit those two; this one completes the set and should pay that setup off);
  Brindisi and the P&O steamer at the end, pointing at Suez for Day 3.
- **Nice:** what Passepartout misses in Paris; the "overland route" and the Indian Mail; the white cliffs; a scrap of
  French and Italian for the language layer.
- **Must not:** treat this as a transit chapter with nothing to see. If a leg has nothing to offer, cut the leg,
  do not pad it.

## Constraints — the lessons Day 1 paid for
- **Density is a hard budget.** Day 1's three failing scenes were failing in English before translation. Target
  **≤ 80 % narration fill** per scene; silence is content. Reject any scene that needs to talk non-stop.
- **Street View is stop-and-look only (D6)**, never transport. Expect at most one or two stops in the whole chapter,
  each with something named to find. Movement is video or hyperlapse.
- **Media fallback ladder first** (`studio/strategy/media-fallback-ladder.md`): freely-licensed video, then open
  street imagery, then stills+motion, then generated. Embed-only YouTube is a last resort and cannot enter the film.
- **Newcomer-first**: assume the traveller has never read the novel. Tell the story before commenting on it.
- **Clear English is the ONLY track** (founder, 2026-09-03 — DECISIONS.md D8, supersedes D5): write it into
  `narration.script`. There is no literary track and no `narration.variants` any more.
- **Tone is a constraint**: unhurried, no cliffhangers, never school.
- Small archive images are fine — the player now mounts them as plates. Do not avoid a good picture for being small.

## Length
Aim ~18–20 min interactive. Fewer, better scenes beats Day 1's count.
