## How things were verified (2026-08-27) — read this before trusting a row

- **Commons items** come from the Commons API (`prop=imageinfo`, `iiprop=url|size|mime|extmetadata`): every width ×
  height, MIME, byte size, duration, licence short-name and author below is the API's answer for that exact file,
  not a guess and not read off a thumbnail. Where the licence mattered I also read the **file page wikitext**
  (licence template + `|source=` + `|author=`) — that is how M-203's provenance and M-227's odd CC BY-SA row were pinned.
- **Video content was watched, not assumed.** M-201, M-202, M-203 and M-205 were downloaded and frame-sampled with
  ffmpeg (timecodes in the notes are the frames I looked at). M-204 and M-206 were **not** watched — they are marked
  *content unverified* and must not be cut in on my word.
- **Mapillary** items come from `graph.mapillary.com/images?bbox=…` with the founder's existing token in
  `www/config.json` (free tier, no account created, no billable SKU). Every id below was resolved to a real
  `thumb_1024_url` and **looked at**. Counts ("290 panos in a 150 m box") are the API's, from a single query.
- **Coordinates** are OSM/Overpass/Nominatim (free): the Fréjus rail-tunnel portal is the endpoint of Overpass way
  `988761574` (`Traforo ferroviario del Frejus`); bearings are haversine between the named points.
- **Street View pano availability could NOT be checked from here.** The founder's key is restricted to Maps Embed +
  referrer (D3), so `streetview/metadata` answers `REQUEST_DENIED` (verified 2026-08-27, five locations). Nothing
  billable was called and nothing was changed. Stops below say "expected — public street; QA confirms in the player",
  exactly as Day 1's second pass did.
- **Not reachable from this environment** (so nobody repeats the attempt): pexels.com **HTTP 403**, pixabay.com
  **HTTP 403**, freesound.org search results are JS-rendered now (individual sound pages still work). Rung-1 modern
  stock video is therefore effectively unavailable to us; everything below at rung 1 is Commons or archive film.
