# World Bible — Around the World in 80 Days

**Source:** Jules Verne, *Le Tour du monde en quatre-vingts jours* (1872 serial / 1873 book). Public domain.
**Frame:** we travel *today*, following Fogg's 1872 route; the novel is our guidebook, not our costume. Present-day footage,
Victorian context layered on top. We may quote the novel; we do not invent events "in the novel".
**Guide persona:** see personas/guide.md. Dialogue personas allowed: Verne (biographical only), Fogg/Passepartout (only what the text supports).
**Route (novel):** London → Suez → Bombay → Calcutta → Hong Kong → Yokohama → San Francisco → New York → London.
**Tone:** brisk, precise, amused — like Fogg's schedule, with Passepartout's wonder.
